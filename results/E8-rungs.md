# E8 — what a consistency rung costs

Generated by `cargo run --release -p experiments -- e8`. Medians over five seeds (1, 7, 42, 100, 2024); 10,000 accounts, 40,000 operations, budget 5%, Zipf *s* = 0.9; identical workload across rungs.

`divergences` compares every served value against an independent fold at the anchor it was served with. A rung that is cheap because it drops deltas is not a cheap rung, and only a value check tells the two apart.

### The table

| rung | deltas applied | maintenance passes | misses | base rows read | divergences |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bounded(k=64) | 2171 | 61 | 26644 | 102624 | 0 |
| bounded(k=8) | 2514 | 446 | 24700 | 73811 | 0 |
| strict(k=0) | 3621 | 4017 | 19658 | 40869 | 0 |

**Reading it.** The rung's saving is in *passes*, not in deltas: a bounded rung is dragged to the frontier less often and folds the same deltas when it is. The read columns are not indistinguishable across rungs — a lax rung misses more and reconstructs more, because its entries are genuinely less current.
